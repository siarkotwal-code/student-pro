📚 Economics & Accounting Terms

Revenue

Income earned from selling goods or services.

Cost

The resources or money used to produce goods or services.

Profit

Revenue minus total costs.

Contribution Margin

Sales revenue minus variable costs.

Gross Margin

Gross profit expressed as a percentage of revenue.

Balance Sheet

Statement showing assets, liabilities and equity.

Income Statement

Statement showing revenue, expenses and profit or loss.
